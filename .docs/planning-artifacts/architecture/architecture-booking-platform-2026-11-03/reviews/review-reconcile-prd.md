# Review: ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md reconciled against the PRD

- **Spine:** `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md` (draft, 2026-11-03)
- **Sources:** `prd.md` (§0–§13), `addendum.md` §A and §I. I also checked `docker-compose.yml` and `src/booking_platform/schema.sql` where a finding depends on what exists.
- **Scope:** the Core (§4.1–4.6, NFR-1..NFR-11, DR-1..DR-5) in full; later phases only where they would force a Core rewrite (§10.3).
- **Date:** 2026-11-03

## Verdict

The spine covers the capacity kernel, transactions, time, sessions, the Outbox, payment and the conventions well. It contradicts the PRD on no hard rule. The gaps are elsewhere. Several Core delivery requirements have no structural home: NFR-1's one-command start and test, the DR-2 sample Bookings, the DR-3 asset register, the DR-1/DR-4 session logs and traceability, and the NFR-8 audit. A few architecture-owned assumptions are also left unset: the anonymous session lifetime, where the Settings live, and the retention scope.

## Findings

Severity: **high** = a Core requirement or a DR item that has no decision and is likely to diverge; **medium** = an architecture-owned item that is unset, or a shape that a later phase would have to rewrite; **low** = a clarification.
