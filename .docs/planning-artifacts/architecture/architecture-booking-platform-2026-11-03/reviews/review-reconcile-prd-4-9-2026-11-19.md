# Review: ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md (AD-23 to AD-26) reconciled against PRD §4.9 Whole-sauna booking

- **Spine:** `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md` (draft, updated 2026-11-19; AD-23 to AD-26 added for §4.9)
- **Sources:** `prd.md` §4.9 (FR-29 to FR-33), and the parts of §1, §2.3 (UJ-5), §3, §4.1, §4.2, §4.4, §5, §7, §10.1, §10.3, §11 (SM-5) and §12 (Q3, Q14) that touch Whole-sauna booking, Placement, Fill order, Private sauna, Cutoff and Room ahead; `addendum.md` §A (Derived Placement, Formulas, spikes) and §J; `appendix-fr-34-51.md` FR-38/FR-39 where §4.9 points to them. I checked `src/booking_platform/schema.sql` (the slice, future `0001`) where a finding depends on it.
- **Date:** 2026-11-19

## Verdict

The spine lands the capacity kernel of §4.9 well (one Booking table, a store-enforced FR-29 predicate on insert and move, one Placement function and one model test), but FR-33 is listed as bound while its Manage-link, refund/Voucher, result-page email and Cutoff-per-Booking parts are missing or deferred, and a handful of server facts and refusal paths (move-target horizon and deadline, refusal codes, what the Private sauna and confirmation responses return, the Guest/Sauna boundary of FR-4) are not yet fixed.

Severity: **high** = the spine contradicts the PRD's plan or claims coverage it does not give, so stories would be written on a wrong basis; **medium** = a testable consequence with no architectural home, or a spine rule that misfires against the PRD; **low** = a clarification, a test-convention gap, or a server fact the SPA needs that a story would probably get right anyway.

## Spine content without a PRD basis

- **AD-23 price comparison against `total_ore`.** See L4. It refuses more than FR-33 asks.
- **AD-24 "every FR-29 refusal is `sauna_unavailable`".** See M2. It overrides AD-16's deadline and horizon codes, and the PRD's FR-29 includes those conditions.
- Nothing else in AD-23 to AD-26 lacks a basis. `kind`/`seats` NULL, the paired capacity row, the deferred pairing check, the in-process Hypothesis machine and the Python brute-force reference in `tests/` are design choices that carry out FR-10, FR-29, FR-31, FR-33 and addendum A without adding behaviour.
