Open Guide: three variants

Same structure and same copy as the chosen design. Three things change: the blue of the primary action, the button design, and the look of Before you come. Pick a variant below, and the Landing and Book seats pages follow.

Reading the mockups: flat blocks stand in for real images, and the system font stands in for Finlandica and Source Sans 3. Hover is shown as an underlined label in all four, since the spine has not defined it yet (finding A-24).

Show on the pages below:

Now: Pale Sky

Button. Square, pale fill, thin dark edge all round.

Before you come. One column of rule groups, with a tall image at the right edge.

Against the spine. This is the current spine.

Primary #B5D2E1, text #1E2D35. Text on fill 9.0:1 (need 4.5). Fill on page 1.4:1 (need 3, or a dark edge).

A: Fjord

Button. Square, solid deep blue, light text, no edge. The fill is strong enough to stand alone, so this is the clearest action of the four.

Before you come. Three columns side by side, with a low, wide image underneath. The whole section can be read at a glance and the page gets shorter.

Against the spine. Fits the spine. Changes the primary and on-primary values, and the button edge is no longer needed. The fill is close to the link colour.

Primary #1D4F73, text #FDFCF7. Text on fill 8.5:1 (need 4.5). Fill on page 7.8:1 (need 3).

B: Harbour

Button. Square, mid blue, no side lines, one heavy dark rule along the bottom, like a stamped block on a printed page.

Before you come. A ledger: the group name on the left and its rules on the right, in the order they are read. The image sits against the rail.

Against the spine. Fits the spine. Changes the primary and on-primary values, and the edge becomes a bottom rule. Dark text on this fill is the tightest pass of the four.

Primary #578FB4, text #10202A. Text on fill 4.7:1 (need 4.5). Fill on page 3.2:1 (need 3).

C: Open Sky

Button. Fully rounded, clear bright blue, thin dark edge. What you press or have picked is round, and everything else stays square.

Before you come. One sheet on the lighter surface, like a card handed over at reception, with Safety given its own column. The side image stays.

Against the spine. Breaks the spine on purpose: square corners everywhere, and the Don't on pills. Choosing it means changing Shapes and that Don't.

Primary #8FCDF0, text #1E2D35. Text on fill 8.2:1 (need 4.5). Fill on page 1.6:1 (need 3, or a dark edge).
